Your first BeyondNet payment

  1. Start and expose this bank over HTTPS.

    Use a WebSocket-capable HTTPS tunnel to localhost:8080. The laptop never joins Bluetooth.

  2. Create accounts while online.

    On each phone choose Personal or Merchant, enter a name, a unique ID ending in @beyondnet and a password. Use the same bank URL and fingerprint below. Existing users can sign in.

  3. Add demo money on the sender.

    Tap Add demo money while connected. New accounts start at zero. No real payment details are needed.

  4. Try an online payment first.

    The merchant shows their QR. The personal user scans it or enters the merchant ID, then authorizes. Live bank receipts appear on both phones automatically.

  5. Try offline payment with two phones.

    Enable Nearby relay on both phones, grant Nearby devices permission and keep Bluetooth and the apps on. Leave the merchant online; turn sender internet off. On the sender scan for nearby phones, tap Connect, then pay. Either account type can relay; no gateway account or switch is needed.

  6. Check the bank receipt.

    Match its reference with Payments and the debit/credit ledger. Add an optional third relay phone to test extra hops. A relay acknowledgment alone is not settlement.
